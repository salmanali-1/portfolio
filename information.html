Featured Project

Flickr30k Caption Preprocessing

This assignment for Mathematical Methods in AI works with Flickr30k, a collection of images paired with written captions. Raw captions are messy, so before any analysis they are cleaned and prepared step by step.

The project builds a 12-stage preprocessing pipeline for the captions and then uses TextBlob to measure the sentiment of the text. It shows how much of AI work is preparing data well before any model sees it.
