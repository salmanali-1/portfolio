Featured Project

Braking System for a Heavy-Duty Automobile

This project was the design of a braking system for a heavy-duty automobile. Heavy vehicles carry large loads and need to stop reliably, so brake components have to be sized and arranged with care, and every part has to fit together in the assembly.

My role was designer. The work involved modelling the brake components in 3D, checking how the parts fit together, and producing mechanical drawings so the design could be understood and reviewed by others.

The project brought together the skills I list on the home page: CAD modelling, technical drafting and a methodical approach to design problems. See the home page for the full list of certificates.
